How It All Began

Our Story

PEHLI MULAQAT
Pehli Nazar Mein…
Ek dawat mein dono ki aankhein mili aur waqt kuch pal ke liye ruk gaya. Shivraj ne kaha — "Sara, tum alag lagti ho sabse." Aur wahi se dono ka safar shuru hua.
DOSTI SE PYAAR
Yaar Bane Humraaz
Mahine beeте, gehri baatein, late-night calls aur dheere dheere dono ne realize kiya ki ye sirf dosti nahi — ye kuch aur hai, kuch khaas.
PROPOSAL
Haan Kar Di Usne!
Picholi Jheel ke kinare, suraj dhalta hua, Shivraj ne ghaatne tek kar poochha — "Meri ho jaaogi?" Sara ki aankhon mein aansu aur hothon pe muskaan. "Haan."
AB SAATH FOREVER
Saat Pheron Ki Kasam
Aur ab 12 April 2025 ko, dono mil ke ek nayi duniya banane wale hain — sath, vishwas aur anant prem ke saath.
